One working day at the coin press. Thirty bells — at each one, strike a coin by hand or buy a machine from the day's catalogue. Only the vault at close counts.

How to play
  1. The mint's day runs to thirty bells. At each bell you do one thing: strike a coin by hand, or buy one machine from the day's catalogue of six.
  2. Machines pay out at every bell — a machine bought this bell starts on the next. What you buy, and when, is everything: a press bought early earns all day; bought late, it never pays for itself.
  3. Only the vault at close counts. Spent coins are gone — every purchase is a bet that the day is long enough to win the money back.
  4. You get three shifts at the press; your best counts, measured against the day's one best plan. Everyone works the same catalogue.
